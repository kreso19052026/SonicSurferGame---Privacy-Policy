SonicSurfer: Audio Rider — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

SonicSurfer: Audio Rider (“SonicSurfer”, “the app”) is a 3D music game for Android made by Kozo Technology. This policy explains what information the app and its advertising partner handle, and the choices you have.

Contact: kozo.technology@gmail.com

Information the app stores on your device

The app keeps your game progress and settings on your device only:

  • the levels you played, your stars, best scores, opened chests, bosses beaten, relics and story progress
  • coins, add-ins, upgrades, the vehicles, paints, trails, decals and themes you unlocked or are trying, and your own custom themes
  • daily challenges, login streak, season pass and event progress, statistics and achievements
  • the ghost of your best run on a level, and tracks you composed in the track maker
  • sound, music, vibration, reduce-motion and graphics settings, and when the last ad between levels was shown

Your own songs: when you tap Import, you pick an audio file (MP3, WAV or AAC) in Android's file picker. The app reads only the file you pick, analyses it on your device to build a road from its beat, and saves a copy in the app's own storage so you can ride it again. Your songs are never uploaded or sent to anyone.

Photos, clips and challenge codes: photo mode pictures, replay clips and friend challenge codes are made on your device. They leave it only when you choose to share them, through Android's share menu, with the app you pick.

The app has no accounts and does not send any of this information to us or anyone else. Uninstalling the app deletes it. Coins are earned by playing and cannot be bought with money; the app has no in-app purchases.

Advertising and your consent

This app shows ads served by Google AdMob: optional rewarded videos that you choose to watch for a stated reward (for example double coins, a revive, free coins, an add-in charge, a theme trial or a few hours without ads), and an occasional full-screen ad between journey levels (never during your first levels). AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address (used for approximate location), device and operating-system information, and ad interaction data to serve and measure ads, and to prevent fraud. See how Google uses this information: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

  • European Economic Area, UK and Switzerland: on first launch the app asks for your consent through Google's certified consent management platform. Personalised ads are shown only if you consent; otherwise you may see limited, non-personalised ads, or no ads.
  • United States: if you live in a US state with a consumer privacy law (such as California), you can opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal information for targeted advertising in Settings → Privacy options.
  • Change your choice at any time: you can change or withdraw consent at any time in the app's Settings → Privacy options. You can also reset your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalisation in your device's Google settings.

The game stays fully playable whatever you choose: every level can be played without watching an ad, and add-ins and revives can also be bought with coins earned by playing.

Permissions

The app uses internet access only to load ads and the consent form; the game itself, its music and your own songs work offline. Vibration is used for haptic feedback and can be turned off in Settings. The other permissions listed on Google Play (network state, advertising ID, keeping the device awake, background work) are added by Google's ads SDK. The app does not ask for your location, contacts, camera or microphone, and it does not read your files except the song you pick yourself.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.
